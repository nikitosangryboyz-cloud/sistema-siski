# Anti-Slop

Derived from the anti-default critique patterns in `Enixes/astra-frontend-design` and `PaulRBerg/agent-skills`.

## Inspect

- Does the design still work if the business subject is replaced with another one?
- Are cards used because content is grouped, or because cards are the default wrapper?
- Are gradients, pills, oversized headings, dividers and decorative labels doing real work?
- Are all sections visually identical?
- Is motion everywhere without hierarchy?
- Are there fake testimonials, fake metrics or invented claims?
- Does every decorative element support hierarchy, function, sequence or subject?

## Our project-specific prohibitions

Avoid purple-blue AI gradients, gradient H1 text, meaningless glassmorphism, random serif/sans combinations, fake AI badges, excessive pills, repetitive equal-card grids and generic "advantages + FAQ" filler when they are not justified by the subject.

Do not remove a visual pattern merely because it is common; remove it when it is unexamined or unsupported by the brief.

## PASS
The site has a clear visual point of view, subject specificity and deliberate use of decorative effects.
